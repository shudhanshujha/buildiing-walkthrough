AURA RESIDENCES — Navigation & Studio Guide

🧭 Exterior & Compass Directions

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, or click N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, Top Plan, or Street Eye on the Compass HUD to inspect sightlines from any orientation.

☀️ Sun & 🌕 Moon Lighting

Open Sun & Moon to scrub 24h daylight/moonlight, play a live timelapse, or manually aim Sun/Moon azimuth (0°–360°) & elevation into any apartment window.

🏢 Floors, Cutaway & 2D Schematic

Each of the 6 floors has a unique layout (Lobby, Smart Suites, Twin 2BHK, 3BHK+1BHK, 4BHK Sky Villa, Crown Penthouse). Toggle Cutaway, Split 3D+2D, or 2D Schematic.

🚶 3D Room Walkthrough

Click Walk 3D on any room, click a room on the 2D plan, or click golden floor rings. Use W A S D / Arrow Keys or the on-screen Directional Pad to walk room-to-room.

🎨 Live Finish Variations

Click directly on any Flooring, Window, Modular Kitchen, Wall, or Furniture in 3D (or open the Customize tab) to swap architectural materials & island layouts live.

📷 Snapshot Studio

Click Capture (or press P) anytime to save high-res PNG renders with optional architectural title-blocks and 2D floorplan insets.
